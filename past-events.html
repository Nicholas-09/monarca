A Look Back at the Moments We've Shared

From educational workshops to cultural celebrations, here's how our community has come together.

Culture

Baile Folklórico Celebration

Families from across East Texas gathered to celebrate Hispanic and Latino culture through dance and music.

Health & Wellness

Mental Health Seminar

A safe space to talk about emotional wellbeing, with practical self-compassion tools participants could take home.

Partnership

Greener CASA Kickoff

Alongside UT Tyler, we launched a program helping families make their homes more efficient and sustainable.

Education

GED Program Graduation

We celebrated adults who completed their Spanish-language GED prep — an achievement shared by the whole community.

Community

Citizenship & Driver's License Day

Volunteers helped dozens of families prepare for key citizenship and driving exams in one community-wide event.

Storytelling

Spanish Storytelling Night

Families shared oral stories and traditions, strengthening bonds across generations.
