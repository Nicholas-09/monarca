Reasons to Give

Why Give?

Direct Impact on Families

Your donation strengthens advocacy and support services, helping East Texas families access essential resources and creating lasting change in their lives.

Community-Led and Trusted

Monarca Initiative is built by and for the community. Over 60% of our volunteers are former participants who now give back through advocacy, literacy, and family support.

Every Dollar Stays Local

Your support strengthens East Texas by ensuring that families thrive right here in our community.

Building equity and opportunity for Hispanic and Latino families across East Texas — through advocacy, education, and community.
