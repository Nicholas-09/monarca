Services

Programs That Meet Families Where They Are

From case management to community seminars, our services are designed alongside Hispanic and Latino families across East Texas.

How We Help

Our Core Programs

Every program is offered bilingually and shaped around real community needs.

Direct Support

Case Management

One-on-one guidance connecting families to housing, health, employment, and other essential resources.

Direct Support

Community Advocacy

We stand alongside families navigating complex systems to make sure their voice is heard and respected.

Health & Wellness

Mental Health Seminars

Workshops that reduce stigma and offer practical tools for emotional wellbeing and self-compassion.

Health & Wellness

Nutrition & Cybersecurity

Practical sessions on healthy eating and protecting personal information online.

Education

GED (Spanish) & ESOL

Spanish-language GED preparation and English for Speakers of Other Languages (ESOL) classes for adults.

Education

Citizenship & Driver's License Prep

Guided prep classes for the citizenship exam and driver's license exam, with bilingual materials.

Culture

Baile Folklórico & Storytelling

Programs celebrating Hispanic and Latino culture through folkloric dance and storytelling in Spanish.

Partnership

Greener CASA

A collaboration with UT Tyler's College of Engineering, helping families make their homes more efficient, sustainable, and affordable.
