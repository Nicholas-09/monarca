Services

Programs That Meet Families Where They Are

From case management to community seminars, our services are designed alongside Hispanic and Latino families across East Texas.

How We Help

Our Programs

Every program is offered bilingually and shaped around real community needs.

Direct Support

Advocacy

Advocacy is at the heart of everything we do. We walk alongside individuals and families, helping them navigate complex systems, access essential resources, and amplify their voices — empowering them to advocate for themselves and create lasting change.

Health & Wellness

Seminars & Workshops

Sessions on mental health, bullying prevention, financial literacy, nutrition, and community resources — each one designed to promote integration and inform, with practical changes participants can put to use right away.

Education

Citizenship

An 8-week course with comprehensive prep for the exam, the interview, and mock interviews. Participants study the 128 civics questions and practice with board member and immigration attorney Ginger Young, who gives personalized feedback.

Education

Practical Skills & Certification Support

Technical and professional training essential for economic growth — including prep for a Driver's License, Food Handling Certificate, Irrigation License, and more.

Education

Spanish GED

Small-group courses that prepare students for the high school equivalency exam, with personalized guidance. The Spanish GED holds the same recognition as the English GED, opening doors to higher education and better jobs.

Education

ESOL

English classes designed for small group settings, led by culturally responsive facilitators who provide an individualized approach that respects each student's background and pace.

Education

Book Club

Our Advanced English course features a Book Club where learners read and discuss selected books together — expanding vocabulary, practicing critical thinking, and deepening understanding of U.S. civics, history, and culture.

Culture

Folkloric Ballet

Ballet Folklórico celebrates the rich cultural heritage of Latin America through traditional dance, music, and vibrant costumes — honoring diverse regional traditions and bringing the spirit of community and history to life.

Partnership

Greener CASA — Home Energy

A collaboration between Monarca Initiative and UT Tyler's College of Engineering that helps Hispanic families transform their homes into more efficient, sustainable spaces — lowering energy costs and strengthening families and community.

Partnership

Bilingual Computer Classes

Learn essential computer skills in Spanish while building key English vocabulary and strengthening digital literacy. Offered through a collaboration between Monarca Initiative and Goodwill Industries of East Texas.
