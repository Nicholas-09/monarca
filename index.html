Who We Are

A Nonprofit Built By & For the Community

Monarca Initiative is a Tyler, Texas-based nonprofit dedicated to removing barriers and advocating for Hispanic and Latino individuals and families across East Texas. More than 60% of our volunteers are former participants who now give back to their community.

Learn More
Services

Programs That Meet Families Where They Are

From case management and advocacy to educational seminars on health, safety, and citizenship, our programs help families thrive throughout East Texas.
