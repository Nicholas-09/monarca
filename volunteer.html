Why It Matters

Volunteers Power Everything We Do

Monarca Initiative was built by volunteers and continues to grow because of them. Many started as program participants and now lead workshops, support other families, and advocate for the community.

Ways to Help

Find the Right Role for You

Whether you have an hour or a day a week, there's a place for you at Monarca Initiative.

Family Support

Help families connect with resources, accompany visits, and offer a familiar, friendly face in the community.

Workshop Facilitation

Share your expertise leading seminars on education, health, or exam preparation topics.

Event Support

Help organize and welcome the community at seminars, cultural celebrations, and community days.

Community Advocacy

Accompany families to appointments and processes, helping make sure their voice is heard.

Tutoring & Literacy

Support GED prep, ESOL classes, or family literacy programs as a one-on-one or group tutor.

Admin & Outreach

Help behind the scenes with community outreach, social media, or administrative tasks.
